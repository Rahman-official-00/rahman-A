05 / The approach

A transparent workflow from problem to working report.

Every engagement has a defined scope, a data-evidence trail and a client-side review point.

STEP 01

Understand the issue

Brief discovery call to establish the symptom, customer requirement, available data and technical decision owner.

STEP 02

Agree scope & inputs

Confirm deliverables, fee, confidentiality expectations and approved/redacted records before analysis begins.

STEP 03

Analyze & document

Review calculations, assumptions, suspected causes and missing evidence. Draft practical action-oriented outputs.

STEP 04

Review & hand over

Discuss findings with authorized client engineers, capture corrections and provide final working documentation.

Safety, integrity and responsibility

Remote consulting does not include on-site inspection, calibrated testing, OEM/IATF certification, final product approval or proven root-cause confirmation without evidence. All implementation, validation and customer approvals remain with the client's authorized team.

06 / Common questions

Useful answers before we work together.

A short conversation is enough to assess whether remote support fits your issue.

Ask about your project ↗
Do you need access to our manufacturing plant?

Not for the listed analysis and documentation services. Your on-site team must collect measurements, perform inspections, trial corrective actions and validate results.

Can you guarantee our complaint will be closed?

No. I can help prepare an evidence-based analysis and review-ready draft, but root-cause verification, effectiveness testing, OEM/customer acceptance and closure remain the client's responsibility.

Can we share confidential drawings or customer data?

Only through a mutually approved confidentiality and data-handling arrangement. Authorized, minimized or de-identified information is preferred. AI tools must not receive restricted files without explicit permission.

Are the portfolio case studies real customer projects?

No. They are explicitly fictional learning demonstrations. They show proposed methods, deliverable styles and calculation approaches—not past client successes.

Is this an OEM or IATF certification service?

No. This practice offers independent quality analysis and working-document support, not accreditation, third-party audits, OEM authorization or certification.

How do payments work?

Following a fit call, the parties agree the fixed scope, schedule, invoice and payment milestones in writing. Any additional work is quoted separately.
