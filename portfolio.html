CASE STUDY 01 • FICTIONAL

Brake booster customer complaint — 8D

Hypothetical situation: intermittent vacuum-related returns after an assembly process. The illustrative shipment volume is 4,500 units.

18Baseline complaints (invented)
4,000Baseline PPM (18 ÷ 4,500 × 1M)
889Proposed scenario PPM (4 ÷ 4,500 × 1M)

Investigation framework

  1. D1/D2: client identifies cross-functional team, failure symptom, traceability and test requirements.
  2. D3: client authorizes containment and suitable inspections.
  3. D4: test hypotheses including seal seating, surface finish, instructions and tester reliability.
  4. D5/D6: evaluate guided insertion, error-proofing and inspection changes subject to plant-side validation.
  5. D7/D8: revise controls only when approved; monitor actual post-action trends.

The change from 4,000 to 889 PPM is a hypothetical comparison, not an achieved reduction. Root cause and closure require client-side evidence and authorized customer approval.

CASE STUDY 02 • SYNTHETIC DATA

Tandem master cylinder bore variation — SPC

Fictional bore feature: nominal 20.000 mm, LSL 19.950 mm, USL 20.050 mm. Synthetic baseline and proposed scenarios each contain 25 subgroups of five measurements.

20.0128 mmBaseline grand mean (simulated)
0.98Illustrative baseline Cpk
2.17Illustrative proposed Cpk

Method: Cpk = min[(USL − mean)/(3σwithin), (mean − LSL)/(3σwithin)]; estimate σwithin via mean subgroup range / d2 with d2 ≈ 2.326 for n=5. Proposed mean is 20.0030 mm; the two illustrative σwithin estimates are 0.0127 and 0.0072 mm.

This figure shows subgroup averages against individual-part specifications for demonstration only. It is not an X-bar/R control chart, and capability claims require a valid measurement system, stable process, distribution assessment and approved control limits.

CASE STUDY 03 • FICTIONAL

PFMEA and MSA evidence review

Sample operation: elastomer seal insertion in an automotive fluid reservoir. Suggestions are working hypotheses for an authorized engineering team to evaluate.

Failure modePossible consequencePossible controls to evaluate
Twisted sealFluid leakageGuided insertion and approved gauge checks
Incorrect sealFit or durability problemSeparate bins, barcode verification and interlock
Incomplete seatingDownstream leakInsertion force window and verified end-stop

MSA evidence checklist

  • Feature specification, resolution, calibration status and measurement method.
  • Number of parts, operators and repetitions; randomized sequence and process-range coverage.
  • Raw readings, method for gauge R&R, number of distinct categories (ndc) and bias/stability assessment.
  • Pre-agreed acceptance criteria and a log of open technical questions.

This is not an official AIAG/VDA FMEA worksheet, certification or validated risk score. Final severity, occurrence, detection and action priorities must be assigned by the client's authorized cross-functional team against applicable requirements.

Professional engagement safeguards

  • Use only authorized, minimized or de-identified data; request client approval before putting restricted content into AI tools.
  • Show assumptions, formulas and evidence gaps in every technical report.
  • Client handles on-site testing, calibration, process trials, product release, customer acceptance and final approvals.
  • No promise of PPM reductions, OEM/IATF certification or safety sign-off without legitimate qualification and evidence.
